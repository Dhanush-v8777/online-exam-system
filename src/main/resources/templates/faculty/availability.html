Manage your eligibility for automated invigilator scheduling.

Status

You will be automatically allocated to examination invigilation slots according to department policy. The scheduling engine will not assign you new invigilation duties until you reactivate your availability.

University Invigilation Guidelines
  • All faculty members must complete a minimum of 4 duties per examination term.
  • Exemptions during active exam periods require approval from the Department Head.
  • Assigned duties cannot be declined within 48 hours of examination start time.
